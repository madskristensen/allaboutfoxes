Some foxes pad across snowy ground. Others listen for tiny footsteps in a
desert. Foxes belong to the dog family, **Canidae**, but each species has its
own ways of finding food, staying comfortable, and raising a family.

Our field guide begins with the red fox: a remarkable neighbor that can make
its home in all sorts of places. Open its field note to explore real photos,
spot its identifying features, and discover the story behind that bushy tail.
