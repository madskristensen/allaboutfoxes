02 / SUPER SENSES
◎

Those ears aren't
just for looks.

A red fox can hear small animals moving out of sight. It listens, tilts its head, and pounces to catch its next meal.

03 / THE FLUFFY STUFF
∿

A built-in
cozy blanket.

A fox's bushy tail helps with balance. When it's time to rest, a fox can curl up and wrap its tail around itself to keep warm.

A question for curious minds: are all foxes red?

No! Arctic foxes can have white coats in winter, and fennec foxes have sandy-colored fur. Even red foxes can have different coat colors. There's a whole world of foxes to discover!

Want to dig deeper? Explore red fox facts from the University of Michigan's Animal Diversity Web.
